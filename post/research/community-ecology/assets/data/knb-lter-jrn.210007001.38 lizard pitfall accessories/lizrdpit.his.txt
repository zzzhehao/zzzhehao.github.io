Variable Descriptions:
DATE - MM/DD/YY (MM = month,  DD = Day, YY = Year)
ZONE - Vegetation zone (C = Creosotebush, G = Grassland,
                        M = Mesquite dune, T = Tarbush)
SITE - Site location (GRAV, SAND, CALI, EAST, WEST, RABB, WELL, IBPE,
                      BASN)
PLOT - Plot A, B, C, D
PIT  - Pit number (1 -16)
SPP  - Species code (4 letter acronym)
COMMENT - Comments from field data books
